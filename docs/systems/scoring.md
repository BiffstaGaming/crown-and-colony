## 1. How it works (plain English)

Your **score** is a single number that rates how well your colony empire is doing. It is the figure the end-of-game victory screen and the high-score table show. You never set it directly — the game adds it up from what you own and what you have achieved, and it changes turn by turn as your nation grows.

**The rules, in plain words:**
- **Your units count — including the colonists working inside your colonies.** Every colonist, soldier, ship and wagon you own is worth a few points — better units are worth more (a free colonist is worth 3, a man-o'-war 8, a petty criminal 1). A colonist toiling in a colony scores exactly as it would standing on the map (an expert farmer working a field is still worth 4).
- **Razing native settlements costs you.** Burning a native settlement is an atrocity: you lose **5 points** for each one, and a further **50 points** if you wipe out the nation's last settlement. (The plunder you take is separate — that adds to your gold.)
- **Finding a City of Gold is remembered.** Stumbling on one of the Seven Cities of Gold is noted in your history; the points come from the treasure it gives you, not from the find itself.
- **Your colonies' loyalty counts.** Each colony adds its banked **liberty** (the bells that drive Sons of Liberty) straight to your score, so a fervent, bell-rich colony is worth a lot.
- **Founding Fathers count.** Each Founding Father you elect to the Continental Congress adds **5 points**.
- **Discovering new lands counts.** The first time you reveal a region nobody has reached, you earn its exploration points — a land region up to ~1000 by size, a mountain range `2 ×` its size, the **Pacific Ocean 100** (see [map & terrain](map-terrain.md) → Region discovery). These accrue through your history log as you explore.
- **Your treasure counts — a little.** Gold converts at **1 point per 1,000 gold** (so 7,500 gold is worth 7 points). Money is the weakest contributor on purpose.
- **Winning independence is the big multiplier.** If you win the War of Independence you get a **percentage bonus on your whole score** — **+100%** for being the first nation to do it (i.e. your score doubles), +50% for the second, +25% for the third.

**Worked example:**
> You have two free colonists (3 + 3) and a caravel (3) on the map — that's **9**. You found a colony and it banks **600 liberty** — **+600**. You elect **two Founding Fathers** — **+10**. You are sitting on **7,500 gold** — **+7** (⌊0.001 × 7500⌋). Your score is **9 + 600 + 10 + 7 = 626**. Later you win independence first, so your **626 doubles to 1,252**.

**What the player sees and does:** the **victory screen** shows the score broken down line-by-line when the game is won, and a **High Scores screen** (opened from the in-game "High Scores" button, and automatically when a game ends) shows a persisted leaderboard of past completed games. The player raises their score by founding colonies, building bells, electing Fathers, accumulating units/gold, and ultimately winning independence.

**The High Scores leaderboard (plain words):**
- When a game ends — you win (defeat the King's army, declare independence) **or** you lose your last colony and units — the game writes your final score to a leaderboard.
- The leaderboard keeps the **top 10** completed games, best score first. Each row shows an **honorific** the score earned (a grand score names a *Continent* after you, a tiny one a *Parasitic Worm* — FreeCol's whimsical naming ladder), your nation, the score, whether you won or lost, the difficulty, how many turns you played, and the date.
- The leaderboard is saved in **its own file** (`highscores.json`), completely separate from your saved games — finishing a game never touches a save, and the leaderboard survives even if you delete all your saves. (This mirrors how the original game keeps high scores in a separate file.)
- A better score from the *same* game replaces the earlier entry rather than listing it twice.

## 2. Detailed rules

The score is recomputed from current state every time it is read (FreeCol `ServerPlayer.updateScore`). The summands, in order:

| Summand | Formula | FreeCol source |
|---|---|---|
| Units | Σ over the player's units of the unit type's `score-value` (read off `unit.Type.ScoreValue`), **plus** Σ over the colonists working inside the player's colonies (`ColonyWorkerScore`) | `sum(getUnits(), Unit::getScoreValue)` (`ServerPlayer.java:860`); `score-value` now **parsed onto `Specification.UnitType`** from `specification.xml` (L1814–2213) and read via `UnitType.ScoreValue` / `Ruleset.UnitScoreValue(id)`, mirroring FreeCol's `UnitType.getScoreValue` (`86d3fpxka`). FreeCol's `getUnits()` includes colony workers; ours adds them explicitly since colony workers are colony population, not unit-list entries (`86d3e4btq`) |
| Colonies | Σ over the player's colonies of `colony.Liberty` | `sum(getColonies(), Colony::getLiberty)` (`ServerPlayer.java:861`) |
| Founding Fathers | `5 × (number of elected fathers)` | `SCORE_FOUNDING_FATHER × count(getFoundingFathers())` (`ServerPlayer.java:862`, constant L165) |
| Gold | `⌊0.001 × gold⌋` (floored) | `(int)Math.floor(SCORE_GOLD × gold)` (`ServerPlayer.java:865`, constant L162) |
| History events | Σ of each history event's stored score — **region discovery** (positive, by region) **plus** the **early-declaration bonus** on the declare-independence event (`max(0, Ruleset.IndependenceTurn − Turn)`, classic independenceTurn 468 — the earlier the break-away, the larger) **minus** the **settlement/nation-destruction penalties** (−5 / −50); lost-city finds are recorded score-less. The human's log only. **Persisted** (save v58). | `score += h.getScore()` (`ServerPlayer.java:881`) — `Game.HistoryEventScore`, added for the human; the DECLARE_INDEPENDENCE early bonus is `csDeclareIndependence`'s `h.setScore(max(0, independenceTurn − turn))` (`86d3c9w2f`, `86d3e4btq`, `86d3fq0dc`) |
| Independence bonus | After the subtotal: `subtotal + subtotal × bonus / 100`, where `bonus` is 100 / 50 / 25 for the 1st / 2nd / 3rd nation to win independence (else 0) | `this.score += (this.score × bonus) / 100` (`ServerPlayer.java:885`); ordinal from the INDEPENDENCE history event's place (L873–877, constants L169–171) |

**Destruction-penalty history events** (recorded on the human attacker, FreeCol `csDestroySettlement`):

| Event | Score | When | FreeCol source |
|---|---|---|---|
| `SettlementDestroyed` | **−5** (`ScoreSettlementDestroyed`) | The human razes a native settlement in a winning settlement assault (`Game.AttackSettlement`) | `SCORE_SETTLEMENT_DESTROYED = -5` (`ServerPlayer.java:156`); `DESTROY_SETTLEMENT` history event (`ServerPlayer.java:3336–3341`) |
| `NationDestroyed` | **−50** (`ScoreNationDestroyed`) | That razing was the nation's **last** settlement (it is wiped out) | `SCORE_NATION_DESTROYED = -50` (`ServerPlayer.java:159`); `DESTROY_NATION` history event (`ServerPlayer.java:3361–3364`) |
| `CityOfGold` | **0** | The human finds a City of Gold (Cibola) Lost City Rumour (`Game.ResolveOutcome`) | `CITY_OF_GOLD` history event with no `setScore` (`ServerUnit.java:580–585`) — its value rides the treasure → gold summand |
| `ColonyDestroyed` | **0** | One of the human's colonies is destroyed (today: starvation) | `COLONY_DESTROYED` history event with no `setScore` (`ServerPlayer.java:3260–3264`) |

(FreeCol's `model.option.destroySettlementScore` *game option* is −2 in the classic ruleset; we pin the code constant `SCORE_SETTLEMENT_DESTROYED = -5` per the task, keeping the penalty a fixed, faithful value.)

| Unit type | Score | | Unit type | Score |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| freeColonist | 3 | | expertFarmer/Fisherman/FurTrapper/SilverMiner/LumberJack/OreMiner | 4 |
| masterSugarPlanter/CottonPlanter/TobaccoPlanter | 5 | | firebrandPreacher, elderStatesman | 6 |
| masterCarpenter | 4 | | masterDistiller/Weaver/Tobacconist/FurTrader/Blacksmith/Gunsmith | 5 |

A unit type with no `score-value` in the ruleset scores **0** (FreeCol's default; e.g. `model.unit.brave`). These numbers are **parsed onto `Specification.UnitType`** (the `score-value` attribute, inherited down the `extends` chain exactly as FreeCol's `getAttribute(SCORE_VALUE_TAG, parent.scoreValue)`), so the table above is data, not code — a variant ruleset retunes the score by editing the spec, no code change.

**Worked edge cases:**

| Input / condition | Result |
|---|---|
| Gold = 999 | `⌊0.999⌋ = 0` points |
| Gold = 1000 | `⌊1.0⌋ = 1` point |
| Gold = 2500 | `⌊2.5⌋ = 2` points |
| Colonist founds a colony | The colonist leaves the unit list (becomes colony population) but is now counted as a **colony worker** — its unit score is unchanged, just relocated; the colony's liberty contributes on top |
| A colony's expert worker (e.g. expert farmer) | Counts in the unit sum at its type's `score-value` (4), not as a free colonist (3) |
| Player is `Colonial` or `Rebel` | No independence bonus (0%) |
| Player is `Independent` (first/only winner) | `+100%` — the whole score doubles |
| Native brave / human-unowned unit | Excluded — only the scored player's own non-native units contribute |
| Human razes a native settlement | −5 (and a further −50 if it was the nation's last settlement) folded into the human's history-event score |
| Human finds a City of Gold | Recorded (`CityOfGold`, score 0); the points come from the treasure (→ gold summand) |

**Deviations from original 1994 / FreeCol behavior:**
- **Colony workers now contribute unit scores** (`86d3e4btq`). FreeCol's `getUnits()` includes colonists working inside colonies; in our model those are colony *population*, not unit-list entries, so `ColonyWorkerScore` sums them explicitly and adds them to the unit summand. Each colony contributes its non-free overlay types (tile workers, building occupants, idle colonists) plus free colonists (score 3) padding out the rest of its `Population`. This closes the old "colony workers don't score" deviation; the same modelling boundary still applies to the continental-army muster (see [independence.md](independence.md)) — only the *score* read counts colony workers, not the muster.
- **History-event scores: all FreeCol summands now counted.** FreeCol folds region-discovery, lost-city-find and settlement/nation-destruction scores into the total via `HistoryEvent.getScore()`. Our `HistoryEvent` carries a numeric `Score`; `Game.HistoryEventScore` sums them and `PlayerScore` adds them **for the human** (the history log is the human's only; a foreign power scores its units/colonies but no history events): **region discovery** (positive, `86d3c9w2f`) and the **settlement/nation-destruction penalties** (−5/−50, `86d3e4btq`). **Lost-city finds** are recorded as score-less `CityOfGold` events — exactly as FreeCol records a score-less `CITY_OF_GOLD`; their value flows through the treasure → gold summand once cashed in. The history log is now **persisted** (save v58), so a reloaded game keeps its discovery score and any destruction penalty (before v58 the log was in-memory only and the discovery score had to be re-earned).
- **Independence ordinal is simplified.** FreeCol stores the 0/1/2 finishing place on the INDEPENDENCE history event. We have a single human player, so an `Independent` nation is necessarily the first to win and takes the 100% bonus; the 50%/25% constants exist for the multi-power future. The ordinal is derived from `DeclaredIndependenceTurn` ordering across independent players so it is already correct should more than one independent nation ever exist.
- **Destruction penalties apply only to the human attacker.** `SCORE_SETTLEMENT_DESTROYED` (−5) and `SCORE_NATION_DESTROYED` (−50) are recorded as history events on the **human** who razes a native settlement (the history log is the human's). An AI razing a settlement docks no score, since the human's log carries only the human's events — faithful to FreeCol's per-player history, given our log is single-player. (`PlayerScore` is typed to allow a negative result; a destruction-heavy game can drive the human's history score below 0.)

## 3. Technical design

**Domain model:** a self-contained "Player score" section on `Game` (`Game.cs`). `Game.ScoreBreakdown(Player)` returns the itemised `ScoreComponents` (a small `readonly record struct` in `ScoreComponents.cs`: the five summands + the independence bonus percent, with `Subtotal`/`IndependenceBonus`/`Total` derived); `Game.PlayerScore(Player)` is now defined as `ScoreBreakdown(player).Total`, so the breakdown is the **single source of truth** and the total can never drift from its parts. `Game.Score` is a human-convenience accessor (`PlayerScore(_human)`) mirroring the existing `Game.Gold`/`Game.Liberty` pattern. A private `IndependenceScoreBonusPercent(Player)` resolves the percentage bonus. The **victory / end-of-game screen** (`VictoryPanel`, `86d3c9xc6`) reads `ScoreBreakdown` to show the winner *why* their score is what it is, line by line — presentation-only (ADR-006), no rules in the panel. No state was added to `Player`: `Player` holds no back-reference to `Game`, so a player-side `Score` property cannot compute the cross-cutting sum (units/colonies live on `Game`) — the read is exposed on `Game` only, where the state lives (ADR-006: rules in engine-free `GameLogic`; presentation reads the oracle).

**Data sources:** the unit `score-value` numbers come from `freecol/data/rules/classic/specification.xml` (L1814–2213) and are **parsed onto `Specification.UnitType`** (`UnitType.ScoreValue`, resolved down the `extends` chain in `Ruleset.ParseUnitTypes` via `ResolveIntAttribute("score-value")`, faithful to FreeCol's `getAttribute(SCORE_VALUE_TAG, parent.scoreValue)`; default 0). The scoring section reads them straight off the type — `UnitScoreValue(Unit)` returns `unit.Type.ScoreValue`, and the colony-worker path looks an id up via `Ruleset.UnitScoreValue(id)` (a tolerant accessor returning 0 for an unknown id, matching `UnitType.getScoreValue`'s 0-default). The former hardcoded `UnitScoreValues` dictionary was **retired** (`86d3fpxka`): the values now live in data, so a variant ruleset's score table needs no code change.

**Algorithms & formulas:** see §2. The C# mirrors `updateScore` line-for-line: integer arithmetic throughout, `Math.Floor` on the gold term, and the percentage bonus applied to the subtotal with integer division (`score += score * bonus / 100`) — bit-identical to FreeCol's `(this.score * bonus) / 100`.

**Integration points:** **none on the turn loop.** Nothing calls `PlayerScore`/`ScoreBreakdown`/`RecordHighScore` during `EndTurn`/AI turns — they are pull-only oracles. The **victory / end-of-game screen** (`VictoryPanel`) calls `ScoreBreakdown` when it renders (opened by `GameController.UpdateVictoryUi` once `Game.Winner` is set). The **high-score leaderboard** is recorded by `GameController.RecordHighScoreIfGameEnded` — called from `RefreshView` after the win/defeat checks, a one-shot per game (`_highScoreRecorded`) so a refresh cannot double-add. Because none of these is invoked inside the turn, they cannot perturb the deterministic stream-0 sequence (ADR-009).

**High-score leaderboard (`86d3c9xb1`).** A faithful port of FreeCol's `HighScore` machinery, split engine/presentation per ADR-006:
- **`HighScore`** (`HighScore.cs`, a `readonly record struct`): one leaderboard entry — player name (the nation's display name; our players carry no free-text name, mirroring FreeCol's `player.getName()` / "anonymous" fallback), nation id, nation-type id, score, the `ScoreLevel` honorific, difficulty, end-of-game unit/colony counts, retirement turn, independence turn (or −1), a won/lost flag, the UTC date, and a per-game id for de-duplication. `ScoreLevel` + `ScoreLevels` port FreeCol's 20-band honorific ladder and its minimum-score thresholds (`Continent` ≥ 40000 … `ParasiticWorm` ≥ 0); `ScoreLevels.ForScore` picks the highest band met, exactly as FreeCol's `find(...)`.
- **`Game.RecordHighScore(player, won, gameId)`**: builds a `HighScore` from current game state — the faithful port of FreeCol's `new HighScore(player)` constructor. Pure and RNG-free (ADR-009): reads the score, units, colonies, player type and difficulty; mutates nothing.
- **`HighScoreStore`** (`HighScoreStore.cs`, pure static): the ranking + serialization layer — `CheckHighScore` (FreeCol `checkHighScore`: same-game dedup, reject negatives, the full-board "beat the worst" rule), `Add` (FreeCol `newHighScore`), `Tidy` (FreeCol `tidyScores`: sort descending, cap at `MaxEntries = 10`), and `ToJson`/`Load` (tolerant of a missing/corrupt file, never throws). String ⇄ list only — **no file I/O** here, so it is engine-free and unit-tested.
- **`HighScoresService`** (presentation): the thin Godot host that wraps the store with `FileAccess` at `user://highscores.json` (the same engine/host split as `GameController` ↔ `SaveGame`).
- **`HighScoresPanel`** (presentation): the screen — handed an already-ranked list (no rules, no file I/O in the panel), it renders a row per entry best-first with the signal-safe `RemoveChild`+`QueueFree` rebuild idiom, mirroring `VictoryPanel`.

**Persistence:** the **score itself** is still *not* saved — it is recomputed from existing persisted state (units, colonies+liberty, congress, gold, player type, independence turn, **and now the persisted history log**) on each read. The **history-event score summand** rides the **history log, which IS persisted from save v58** (`86d3e4btq`): `SaveGame.History` (a `SavedHistoryEvent[]` of kind/turn/description/score) is written by `SaveGame.From` (omitted when the log is empty), re-appended by `Game.RestoreHistory` on load, so the discovery score and any destruction penalty survive a save/load round-trip rather than being re-earned/lost. The field is **additive + omit-when-empty**: a game whose log is empty serialises byte-identically to v57, and pre-v58 saves load with an empty log (then accrue events as before). The score read itself still adds no save state — only the history log it sums is now saved.

The **high-score leaderboard** persists to its **own** JSON document, `user://highscores.json`, written by `HighScoresService` — **entirely separate from the game save**, exactly as FreeCol keeps high scores in their own file rather than inside a saved game. Recording a score therefore never touches a save and **bumped no save version** (it was v52 at that slice; the current save version is v58, bumped by unrelated slices including this one). The per-game id used for de-duplication is a fresh GUID minted at `StartGame` and is **not** stored in the save (it would need a save-version bump); a reloaded game thus gets a new id and can record a second leaderboard entry — an accepted, documented limitation of not bumping the save.

**Purity / determinism (ADR-009):** `PlayerScore` draws **no randomness** (it constructs no generator and calls none) and **mutates no state** — it only reads the unit/colony lists, the player fields, and the (already-accrued) history log; verified by a test asserting `game.RandomState` and the unit/colony counts are unchanged across two reads. Region **discovery** (which *populates* the history score) is likewise RNG-free and happens at fog-reveal time, not at score-read time, so a score read remains a pure pull-only oracle.
