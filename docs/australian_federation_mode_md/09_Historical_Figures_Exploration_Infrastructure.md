# Historical Figures — Exploration & Infrastructure

## Matthew Flinders

**Category:** Exploration & Infrastructure  
**Earliest availability:** 1801+  
**Suggested cost:** 350 Exploration points

### Prerequisites

- Player owns or charters an ocean-going vessel.
- At least 20 coastal tiles remain unrevealed OR map-completion objective active.
- No full coastline map purchased/imported.

### Perk — Complete Coastline

- Reveals large sections of Australian coastline.
- Ships gain +1 sight.
- Coastal settlements can be founded one tile farther from known ports.
- Coastal resource discovery chance increased.

### Linked events

- `event.coastal_circumnavigation`
- `event.charting_the_continent`

### Story / balance notes

Flinders is the coastline-mapping figure. Best used early to mid-game to accelerate port planning and coastal settlement.

## Charles Sturt

**Category:** Exploration & Infrastructure  
**Earliest availability:** 1828+  
**Suggested cost:** 450 Exploration points

### Prerequisites

- Inland river system discovered OR river expedition unit available.
- At least one settlement is near a major river.
- Player has Scout, Surveyor, or Boat/Canoe capability.

### Perk — River Highways

- River movement cost reduced.
- River settlements gain +1 Food.
- River trade routes produce +15% trade value.
- Unlocks `River Expedition` event pool.

### Linked events

- `event.murray_darling_expedition`
- `event.river_trade_routes`

### Story / balance notes

Sturt converts inland rivers into logistics corridors. This makes river settlement powerful but should still be limited by drought and Country pressure.

## Ludwig Leichhardt

**Category:** Exploration & Infrastructure  
**Earliest availability:** 1844+  
**Suggested cost:** 500 Exploration points

### Prerequisites

- Northern or inland exploration route available.
- Naturalist, Scout, or Surveyor unit present.
- At least one unexplored tropical or savanna region exists.

### Perk — Naturalist Journals

- Scouts gain +2 sight.
- Exploration rumours are 25% less likely to be negative.
- Natural resource discoveries grant extra Exploration points.
- Tropical expedition supply penalty reduced by 25%.

### Linked events

- `event.port_essington_expedition`
- `event.lost_expedition_risk`

### Story / balance notes

Leichhardt should be high-reward but risky. Consider a linked `Lost Expedition` risk if the player overextends without supply depots.

## John McDouall Stuart

**Category:** Exploration & Infrastructure  
**Earliest availability:** 1862+  
**Suggested cost:** 650 Exploration points

### Prerequisites

- Central route has been partly surveyed OR inland desert barrier blocks expansion.
- At least one expedition has crossed arid terrain.
- Player has water/supply logistics capability.

### Perk — North-South Crossing

- Arid and desert movement penalties reduced by 50% for scouts/surveyors.
- Reveals a viable north-south overland route.
- Unlocks `Overland Route Survey`.
- Telegraph construction along surveyed route costs 20% less.

### Linked events

- `event.south_north_crossing`
- `event.overland_route_survey`

### Story / balance notes

Stuart should bridge exploration and telegraph infrastructure. His perk should not make desert settlement easy; it should make route planning feasible.

## Charles Todd

**Category:** Exploration & Infrastructure  
**Earliest availability:** 1872+  
**Suggested cost:** 800 Infrastructure points

### Prerequisites

- North-south route surveyed.
- Telegraph Wire or Telegraph Office technology/building available.
- At least two colony regions require communication connection.
- Stuart or equivalent route condition completed.

### Perk — Electric Telegraph

- Telegraph-connected colonies generate +25% Civic Voice.
- Intercolonial event delays reduced.
- Federation Support decay from distance halved.
- Unlocks `Telegraph Office` and `Overland Telegraph Completed` event.

### Linked events

- `event.overland_telegraph_completed`
- `event.europe_connected_by_wire`

### Story / balance notes

Todd is the major communication-infrastructure figure. He should be one of the best late-game accelerators for Federation Support.
