## Detailed event cards

### Sydney Cove Established

**Earliest:** 1788  
**Type:** Scenario setup  
**Forced:** Yes, in historical campaign only.

Prerequisites:

- Historical British campaign.
- New South Wales start selected.

Effects:

- Creates first settlement at Sydney Cove / Warrane area.
- Grants Government Stores, limited Food, Tools, and convict labour.
- Sets initial relationship state with Eora/Dharug/Dharawal regional context.

### First Contact Around Warrane

**Earliest:** 1788  
**Type:** Regional / diplomacy  
**Forced:** Yes in historical campaign, conditional in sandbox.

Prerequisites:

- Settlement founded in Sydney region.
- Eora/Dharug/Dharawal group active.

Effects:

- Opens First Nations diplomacy UI.
- Introduces Respect, Tension, and Country Pressure.
- Offers peaceful contact, cautious distance, or aggressive expansion choices.

### Starvation Crisis

**Earliest:** 1788  
**Type:** Crisis  
**Forced:** No.

Prerequisites:

- Food storage below 3 turns of consumption.
- No stable farm or fish supply.
- Early era.

Effects:

- Population growth halts.
- Morale falls.
- Emergency ration choice appears.
- Arthur Phillip or James Ruse reduce severity.

### Smallpox in Sydney Basin

**Earliest:** 1789  
**Type:** Historical hardship / sensitive event  
**Forced:** Optional historical-events setting only.

Prerequisites:

- Historical mode.
- Sydney region contact established.

Recommended handling:

- Do not reward the player.
- Use narrative text and legitimacy consequences.
- Reduce nearby First Nations community stability only if the game has a serious disease model.
- Add memorial/truth-telling entry to historical log.

### Experiment Farm

**Earliest:** 1789  
**Type:** Figure-linked  
**Forced:** No.

Prerequisites:

- James Ruse attained.
- Farm tile available.
- Food shortage or self-sufficiency objective active.

Effects:

- Free Expert Farmer.
- Farm yield bonus.
- Food crisis chance reduced.

### Merino Sheep Introduced

**Earliest:** 1797  
**Type:** Economy  
**Forced:** No.

Prerequisites:

- Elizabeth Macarthur attained OR sheep import route exists.
- Suitable pasture.

Effects:

- Unlocks Sheep Station.
- Wool economy begins.
- Country Pressure from pastoral expansion increases if unmanaged.

### Macquarie Governorship

**Earliest:** 1810  
**Type:** Figure-linked administration  
**Forced:** No.

Prerequisites:

- Lachlan Macquarie attained.
- Year at least 1810.
- Colony population 5+.
- Public works backlog or road shortage.

Effects:

- Public Works Program option.
- Road/building discounts.
- Emancipist integration improvement.

### Gold Rush Begins

**Earliest:** 1851  
**Type:** Economy / crisis  
**Forced:** No.

Prerequisites:

- Gold potential discovered OR Edward Hargraves attained.
- Hill/mountain region explored.

Effects:

- Goldfield appears.
- Immigration surge.
- Inflation and unrest increase.
- Peter Lalor becomes more likely if licence pressure rises.

### Eureka Stockade

**Earliest:** 1854  
**Type:** Crisis / reform  
**Forced:** No.

Prerequisites:

- Goldfield active.
- Mining licence unrest high.
- Peter Lalor attained OR reform pressure above threshold.

Effects:

- Short-term unrest and possible conflict.
- Unlocks Miner’s Right reform path.
- Civic Voice increases if reform path chosen.

### Overland Telegraph Completed

**Earliest:** 1872  
**Type:** Infrastructure  
**Forced:** No.

Prerequisites:

- Charles Todd attained.
- North-south route surveyed.
- Telegraph Wire stockpile.
- At least two colony regions connected.

Effects:

- Telegraph network bonus.
- Federation Support decay from distance reduced.
- Communication delay to Europe reduced.

### Tenterfield Oration

**Earliest:** 1889  
**Type:** Federation  
**Forced:** No.

Prerequisites:

- Henry Parkes attained.
- Federation Support system visible.
- At least four colony regions active.
- Civic Voice threshold met.

Effects:

- +10 Federation Support in all active colonies.
- Unlocks Federation League Hall.
- Starts Federation Movement phase if not already started.

### Corowa Conference

**Earliest:** 1893  
**Type:** Federation process  
**Forced:** No.

Prerequisites:

- John Quick attained.
- Federation League or civic conference infrastructure.
- At least one colony above 60 support.

Effects:

- Convention costs reduced.
- Elected delegates mechanic unlocked.

### First Federation Referendums

**Earliest:** 1898  
**Type:** Victory sequence  
**Forced:** No.

Prerequisites:

- Draft Constitution progress at least 80%.
- At least four colonies above 50 support.
- Federation Convention completed.

Effects:

- Holds referendums in eligible colonies.
- If NSW support passes but mobilisation quota fails, triggers revision path.

### Second Federation Referendums

**Earliest:** 1899  
**Type:** Victory sequence  
**Forced:** No.

Prerequisites:

- First referendum failed or partial.
- Constitution revised.
- Key support targets improved.

Effects:

- Final eastern-colony referendum opportunity.

### Western Australia Referendum

**Earliest:** 1900  
**Type:** Victory sequence  
**Forced:** No.

Prerequisites:

- WA politically active.
- WA support at least 55, or 70 for direct pass.
- Goldfields pressure, telegraph/port connection, or trade benefits active.

Effects:

- WA joins Federation if target reached.
- If failed, optional five-colony victory setting can continue.

### Commonwealth Proclamation

**Earliest:** 1901  
**Type:** Victory  
**Forced:** No.

Prerequisites:

- Required colonies have passed.
- Constitution completed.
- Imperial assent step completed.

Effects:

- Victory.
- Shows victory grade and unresolved historical notes.
