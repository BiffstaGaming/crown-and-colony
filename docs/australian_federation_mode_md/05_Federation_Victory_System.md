# Federation Victory System

## Purpose

This system replaces the FreeCol War of Independence with a political, constitutional, and regional support victory.

## Core resources

### Civic Voice

Equivalent to Liberty Bells. Generated by:

- Town Halls.
- Newspapers.
- Schools.
- Courts.
- Federation League Halls.
- Convention Halls.
- Historical Figure perks.
- Reform events.

### Federation Support

A percentage in each colony region. Equivalent to Sons of Liberty support, but tracked per colony.

### Anti-Federation Sentiment

Equivalent to Tory sentiment, but can represent:

- Imperial loyalism.
- Local colony autonomy concerns.
- Tariff fears.
- Small-state representation fears.
- Western Australian distance concerns.
- Labour or immigration-policy concerns.

### Convention Points

Used to unlock constitutional clauses and Federation Convention stages.

## Colony regions

The six referendum regions:

1. New South Wales.
2. Victoria.
3. Queensland.
4. South Australia.
5. Tasmania.
6. Western Australia.

## Federation support targets

Suggested thresholds based on the final historical referendum outcomes:

| Colony | Suggested target | Gameplay identity |
|---|---:|---|
| New South Wales | 57% | Hardest large-colony political negotiation. |
| Queensland | 56% | Regional distance and pastoral concerns. |
| South Australia | 80% | Reform-minded but still needs civic work. |
| Tasmania | 94% | Small colony, high support but wants representation guarantees. |
| Victoria | 94% | Strong support, gold/economic confidence. |
| Western Australia | 70% | Late, distant, hesitant, may need goldfields pressure. |

## Victory phases

### Phase 1 — Colonial Maturity

Requirements:

- At least 4 colony regions have a capital-level settlement.
- At least 3 colony regions have Town Hall or equivalent.
- At least 2 intercolonial trade routes exist.
- Overall Civic Voice generation meets minimum threshold.

Unlocks:

- Federation Support visible.
- Federationist / Anti-Federationist population labels.
- Federation-themed Historical Figures.

### Phase 2 — Federation Movement

Requirements:

- Year at least 1889 OR Henry Parkes attained OR Civic Voice reaches a high threshold.
- At least 4 colony regions discovered/settled.
- At least 2 newspapers active.

Unlocks:

- Federation League Hall.
- Tenterfield Oration event pool.
- Federation Campaigner unit.

### Phase 3 — Convention Process

Requirements:

- At least 4 colonies above 40% Federation Support.
- Convention Points available.
- At least one Democracy & Federation Historical Figure attained.

Unlocks:

- Constitutional clause drafting.
- Convention delegate events.
- John Quick / Samuel Griffith / Edmund Barton chains.

### Phase 4 — Draft Constitution

Drafting requires clauses. Suggested clause categories:

| Clause | Gameplay effect | Common opposition |
|---|---|---|
| Senate Equality | Small colonies gain support; large colonies lose small amount. | NSW/Victoria may dislike. |
| Intercolonial Free Trade | Trade bonus; tariff revenue loss. | Protectionist colonies. |
| Defence Union | Defence costs reduced; imperial oversight shifts. | Isolationist regions. |
| High Court | Dispute resolution; legal admin bonus. | High bureaucracy cost. |
| Railway Standardisation | Long-term infrastructure bonus; high immediate cost. | Budget opposition. |
| Immigration Policy | Support from some groups; legitimacy penalties if exclusionary. | Ethical and diplomatic controversy. |
| Capital Compromise | NSW/Victoria dispute resolution. | Requires funds and planning. |

### Phase 5 — Referendums

A referendum can be called when:

- Each colony has at least 50% Federation Support OR Edmund Barton has been attained and special conditions are met.
- Draft Constitution completion is at least 80%.
- At least 4 colonies have active newspapers or town halls.
- No colony has frontier legitimacy crisis above critical threshold.

### Phase 6 — Failure and Retry

A failed referendum should not end the game. It should create:

- Support loss in failed colonies.
- Constitution revision demands.
- Temporary anti-Federation momentum.
- New event options to negotiate compromise.

John Quick and Samuel Griffith should reduce the cost of recovery.

## Optional historical rule: NSW quota failure

The 1898 New South Wales referendum received majority support but did not meet the required quota. In game terms:

- NSW requires both 57% support and a minimum turnout/civic mobilisation threshold.
- If support passes but mobilisation fails, the referendum fails only in NSW.
- The player must run a second campaign or accept constitutional compromise.

## Western Australia late-entry rule

Western Australia should be eligible to join late.

Suggested condition:

- If five colonies pass, WA can hold a late referendum if:
  - WA support is at least 55%, and
  - Goldfields or eastern-settler pressure is active, or
  - Telegraph/port connection exists, or
  - John Forrest-like regional event is completed.

## Victory conditions

To win:

- All six colonies pass their target OR optional five-colony victory is enabled.
- Constitution is drafted.
- Commonwealth Proclamation event is triggered.
- Imperial assent / enabling act step completed.

## Optional victory grades

| Grade | Requirements |
|---|---|
| Bare Federation | All colonies pass, but legitimacy or First Nations relations poor. |
| Stable Commonwealth | All colonies pass, strong infrastructure, low debt. |
| Reform Commonwealth | Women's suffrage/reform clauses active, high civic institutions. |
| Treaty Commonwealth | Strong First Nations agreements and low frontier violence. |
| Economic Commonwealth | Strong intercolonial trade, rail/telegraph, export economy. |
