# Historical Figures — Settlement, Administration & Defence

## Arthur Phillip

**Category:** Settlement, Administration & Defence  
**Earliest availability:** 1788+  
**Suggested cost:** Starter / 250 Administration points

### Prerequisites

- Historical campaign start OR first settlement founded.
- Government Stores not yet established OR early food risk active.

### Perk — Survival Rations

- First settlement receives emergency Food and Tools.
- First settlement cannot starve below population 2 for 10 turns.
- Unlocks `Government Stores`.
- Initial First Contact tension rises more slowly for 10 turns if no encroachment occurs.

### Linked events

- `event.sydney_cove_established`
- `event.survival_rations_issued`
- `event.first_contact_around_warrane`

### Story / balance notes

Phillip can be a starter figure in historical mode or an early administrative figure in sandbox mode. Keep the perk focused on survival, not conquest.

## Lachlan Macquarie

**Category:** Settlement, Administration & Defence  
**Earliest availability:** 1810+  
**Suggested cost:** 550 Administration points

### Prerequisites

- Year is at least 1810.
- At least one colony has population 5+.
- Government Stores or Town Hall exists.
- Public works demand, road shortage, or emancipist integration issue exists.

### Perk — Public Works Governor

- Colonies of population 3+ receive one free road on an adjacent worked tile.
- Public buildings cost 20% fewer tools/hammers.
- Unlocks `Public Works Program` policy.
- Emancipist units suffer reduced civic penalty.

### Linked events

- `event.macquarie_governorship`
- `event.public_works_program`
- `event.emancipist_integration`

### Story / balance notes

This is the example of a non-forced historical event. Macquarie Governorship should only become eligible after the player attains Macquarie and the year/prerequisites match.

## James Ruse

**Category:** Settlement, Administration & Defence  
**Earliest availability:** 1789+  
**Suggested cost:** 300 Food/Administration points

### Prerequisites

- First farm established OR food deficit occurred.
- At least one settlement has suitable farm tile.
- Convict Labourer or Emancipist available.

### Perk — Experiment Farm

- All farms produce +1 Food.
- One settlement receives a free Expert Farmer.
- Farm improvement tool cost reduced by 25%.
- Starvation-crisis event severity reduced.

### Linked events

- `event.experiment_farm`
- `event.first_self_sufficient_farm`

### Story / balance notes

Ruse is a food-security figure. Useful for early survival and for turning convict labour into productive agriculture.

## Mary Reibey

**Category:** Settlement, Administration & Defence  
**Earliest availability:** 1817+  
**Suggested cost:** 450 Commerce/Administration points

### Prerequisites

- At least one port or coastal trade route.
- Market, Wharf, or merchant ship exists.
- Emancipist policy active OR former-convict social mobility event occurred.

### Perk — Merchant Fleet

- Coastal trade routes produce +25% value.
- First Merchant Ship costs 50% less.
- Ports generate +1 Commerce point per turn.
- Emancipist merchants produce normal trade value without civic penalty.

### Linked events

- `event.emancipist_merchant_network`
- `event.coastal_trade_expansion`

### Story / balance notes

Reibey adds early commerce and social mobility flavour. Her perk supports a coastal Australian economy without waiting for late infrastructure.

## William Jervois

**Category:** Settlement, Administration & Defence  
**Earliest availability:** 1877+  
**Suggested cost:** 600 Defence/Administration points

### Prerequisites

- At least two coastal colonies.
- Imperial defence concern, naval scare, or coastal vulnerability event active.
- Harbour, Wharf, or Battery building available.

### Perk — Harbour Batteries

- Coastal colonies of population 3+ gain a free Battery or Stockade-equivalent.
- Defence buildings cost 30% less.
- Imperial pressure from defence concerns reduced by 20%.
- Ports with Harbour Battery resist raids/blockades better.

### Linked events

- `event.colonial_defence_review`
- `event.harbour_batteries_constructed`

### Story / balance notes

Jervois replaces some of Colonization’s military fathers with a defensive, port-focused Australian equivalent.
