# Historical Figures — Democracy & Federation

## Henry Parkes

**Category:** Democracy & Federation  
**Earliest availability:** 1889+  
**Suggested cost:** 700 Democracy points

### Prerequisites

- Year is at least 1889 OR Civic Voice is very high.
- At least four colony regions are politically active.
- At least two newspapers or town halls exist.
- Federation Support system visible.

### Perk — Tenterfield Oration

- All colony regions gain +10 Federation Support immediately.
- Unlocks `Federation League Hall`.
- Federation Campaigner units become recruitable.
- Anti-Federation sentiment from apathy reduced by 10.

### Linked events

- `event.tenterfield_oration`
- `event.federation_leagues_form`

### Story / balance notes

Parkes should be the big unlock for the Federation era. If the player has already built huge support, the event can instead grant Convention Points.

## Edmund Barton

**Category:** Democracy & Federation  
**Earliest availability:** 1897+  
**Suggested cost:** 900 Democracy points

### Prerequisites

- Draft Constitution progress at least 50%.
- At least four colonies above 50 Federation Support.
- Convention Hall exists OR National Convention event completed.
- At least one failed or pending referendum stage.

### Perk — Nation for a Continent

- Final Referendum can be called once all colonies exceed 50 support, even if some have not hit target yet.
- Failed referendum recovery is 25% faster.
- New South Wales support target reduced by 3 points.
- Convention Points generation +25%.

### Linked events

- `event.national_australasian_convention`
- `event.final_referendum_campaign`

### Story / balance notes

Barton is the closer figure. His perk should not auto-win; it should let the player enter the final sequence earlier with political risk.

## John Quick

**Category:** Democracy & Federation  
**Earliest availability:** 1893+  
**Suggested cost:** 750 Democracy points

### Prerequisites

- Federation League Hall or equivalent civic organisation exists.
- At least one colony has 60+ support.
- At least one colony has failed to reach a convention/referendum threshold OR Corowa-style conference condition active.

### Perk — Corowa Plan

- Convention costs reduced by 25%.
- Failed referendum penalties are halved.
- Unlocks `Elected Delegates` mechanic.
- Small towns can contribute limited Convention Points.

### Linked events

- `event.corowa_conference`
- `event.elected_convention_plan`

### Story / balance notes

Quick is the process designer. He should make failed Federation attempts recoverable and bring smaller towns into the national debate.

## Samuel Griffith

**Category:** Democracy & Federation  
**Earliest availability:** 1891+  
**Suggested cost:** 800 Democracy/Administration points

### Prerequisites

- Legal institutions exist in at least three colony regions.
- Court House or administrative building exists.
- Constitution drafting has begun OR Convention process unlocked.

### Perk — Draft Constitution

- Constitution progress immediately increases by 30%.
- The hardest colony support target is reduced by 5 points.
- Constitutional clause costs reduced by 15%.
- Intercolonial legal disputes resolve faster.

### Linked events

- `event.draft_constitution_prepared`
- `event.constitutional_compromise`

### Story / balance notes

Griffith is the constitutional drafting figure. He should help solve mechanics around legal clauses, small-state fear, and federal structure.

## Catherine Helen Spence

**Category:** Democracy & Federation  
**Earliest availability:** 1894+  
**Suggested cost:** 700 Democracy/Reform points

### Prerequisites

- South Australia active OR proportional representation reform debate unlocked.
- Newspaper, school, or reform society exists.
- At least one small colony has representation concern.
- Civic reform score above threshold.

### Perk — Fair Representation

- Small colonies generate +25% Federation Support.
- Unlocks `Proportional Representation` reform option.
- Senate Equality clause costs 20% less.
- Women reform events gain +10 success chance.

### Linked events

- `event.proportional_representation_campaign`
- `event.reform_candidate_for_convention`

### Story / balance notes

Spence links democracy, representation, and Federation design. She is ideal for solving small-colony fears without brute-forcing support.
