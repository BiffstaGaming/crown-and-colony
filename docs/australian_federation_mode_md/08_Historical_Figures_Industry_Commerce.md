# Historical Figures — Industry & Commerce

## Elizabeth Macarthur

**Category:** Industry & Commerce  
**Earliest availability:** 1797+  
**Suggested cost:** 350 Industry points

### Prerequisites

- Sheep or wool has been imported, OR year is 1797+.
- At least one pasture/sheep station or suitable grassland tile.
- Colony has stable food supply.

### Perk — Merino Bloodlines

- Sheep Stations produce +50% Wool.
- Unlocks `Merino Stud` improvement.
- Wool Sheds produce +1 extra Wool per turn.
- First active sheep station receives 20 free Sheep.

### Linked events

- `event.merino_sheep_introduced`
- `event.wool_export_boom`

### Story / balance notes

Use as the first major pastoral economy figure. The bonus should be strong but dependent on land and transport, so wool does not automatically win the game.

## Thomas Sutcliffe Mort

**Category:** Industry & Commerce  
**Earliest availability:** 1843+; stronger after 1860  
**Suggested cost:** 650 Industry points

### Prerequisites

- At least one developed port.
- Wool, meat, fish, or dairy export chain active.
- Market or warehouse building present.
- Refrigeration research/event pool unlocked after 1860.

### Perk — Cold Stores

- Unlocks `Freezing Works`.
- Meat, Fish, and Dairy exports gain +50% value if shipped through a Freezing Works port.
- Perishable-goods spoilage is removed on connected trade routes.
- Ports with Freezing Works generate +1 Industry point per turn.

### Linked events

- `event.frozen_meat_export_trial`
- `event.export_refrigeration_breakthrough`

### Story / balance notes

This is a late economic logistics perk. It should turn pastoral expansion into export value, but require ports and infrastructure.

## George Fife Angas

**Category:** Industry & Commerce  
**Earliest availability:** 1836+  
**Suggested cost:** 500 Industry or Administration points

### Prerequisites

- South Australia active OR free-settlement policy unlocked.
- Bank, credit office, port, or immigration office present.
- Debt, capital shortage, or colony-founding cost pressure exists.

### Perk — Colonial Credit

- Immediately clears one debt, credit squeeze, or boycott-style trade penalty.
- Rush-buy cost for buildings reduced by 20%.
- Free-settlement colonies receive +10% immigration growth.
- South Australia gains +5 Federation Support once Federation system is visible.

### Linked events

- `event.south_australia_founded`
- `event.colonial_credit_scheme`

### Story / balance notes

Angas is the planned-colony/finance figure. He should help players recover from slow settlement or fund expansion without becoming a pure money printer.

## Edward Hargraves

**Category:** Industry & Commerce  
**Earliest availability:** 1851+  
**Suggested cost:** 700 Industry points

### Prerequisites

- Year is at least 1851 OR mineral survey finds gold potential.
- At least one explored hill/mountain region.
- Player has Surveyor, Scout, or Digger capability.

### Perk — Payable Gold

- Reveals 2–4 Gold deposits on eligible explored hill/mountain tiles.
- Triggers one major immigration surge.
- Unlocks `Goldfield` improvement and `Digger` unit.
- Gold price becomes volatile for 20 turns.

### Linked events

- `event.gold_rush_begins`
- `event.goldfield_migration_surge`
- `event.chinese_miners_on_the_diggings`

### Story / balance notes

Hargraves should be a boom-and-instability figure. Gold should bring labour, wealth, unrest, inflation, and civic reform pressure. Historical notes should avoid overstating sole discovery credit.

## Sidney Kidman

**Category:** Industry & Commerce  
**Earliest availability:** 1880+  
**Suggested cost:** 650 Industry points

### Prerequisites

- At least two pastoral stations.
- Inland route or stock route exists.
- Drought or long-distance logistics pressure has occurred.
- Cattle or Sheep economy active.

### Perk — Overland Stations

- Drovers, wagons, and stock units gain +1 movement on plains, grassland, and arid tiles.
- Drought penalties to connected pastoral stations reduced by 50%.
- Unlocks `Stock Route` improvement.
- Pastoral stations connected by stock routes generate +1 Industry point.

### Linked events

- `event.overland_station_network`
- `event.drought_management`

### Story / balance notes

Kidman is a late pastoral logistics figure. He should make inland networks viable without eliminating the ecological and diplomatic costs of pastoral expansion.
