## Victory text

### Basic victory

> The colonies have voted to federate. The Commonwealth of Australia is proclaimed, and the continent’s colonial governments enter a new constitutional era.

### Historically honest addendum

> Federation joins the colonies, but it does not resolve every question. Many Aboriginal and Torres Strait Islander peoples and other communities were excluded from the political settlement. Country, sovereignty, representation, and justice remain unfinished parts of the national story.

## Failure text

### Referendum failure

> The Federation campaign has failed to win enough support. Rival colonies question the terms, local interests resist change, and the proposed Constitution must be revised before another vote can succeed.

### Low legitimacy victory warning

> Federation has been achieved, but at severe cost. Frontier conflict, broken agreements, and exclusionary policies have left the Commonwealth unstable and morally compromised.

## Suggested game glossary

| Term | Definition |
|---|---|
| Civic Voice | Public political activity that builds Federation Support. |
| Federation Support | Percentage of a colony’s population/political class supporting Federation. |
| Convention Points | Political capital used to draft constitutional clauses. |
| Country Pressure | Colonial impact on First Nations Country. |
| Respect | Trust and positive relationship with First Nations groups. |
| Tension | Risk of conflict or breakdown in relations. |
| Agreement | Negotiated arrangement with First Nations communities. |
| Historical Figure | A major person whose ideas/actions unlock a permanent game effect. |

## Tone examples

### Good

- “First Nations communities on this Country warn that pastoral expansion is damaging water and food sites.”
- “Federationists argue that a single customs system will strengthen intercolonial trade.”
- “The colony’s newspapers call for reform after the goldfield licence crisis.”

### Avoid

- “Native village conquered.”
- “Tribe eliminated.”
- “Civilise the natives.”
- “Empty land discovered.”
- “Aborigines converted into workers.”
