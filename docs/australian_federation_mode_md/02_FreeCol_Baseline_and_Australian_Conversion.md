# FreeCol Baseline and Australian Conversion

## What FreeCol currently does

FreeCol's Colonization model is built around:

- European powers founding colonies in the Americas.
- Goods production, trade, immigration, and settlement growth.
- Native settlements that can trade, teach skills, offer missions, or become hostile.
- Liberty Bells converting Tory sentiment into Sons of Liberty support.
- Founding Fathers elected into the Continental Congress by accumulating points.
- A final independence declaration and war against the Royal Expeditionary Force.

## Core conversion table

| FreeCol / Colonization concept | Australian Federation Mode concept | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| New World | Australian continent | Main campaign map. |
| European crown | British imperial authority | Historical campaign only. |
| Colonies | Australian colonial settlements | Later grouped into colony regions. |
| Founding Fathers | Historical Figures | Same gameplay function, different framing. |
| Continental Congress | Federation Convention | Unlocks constitutional/federation milestones. |
| Liberty Bells | Civic Voice / Federation Support | Generated by town halls, newspapers, schools, conventions. |
| Sons of Liberty | Federationists | Supporters of Federation or self-government. |
| Tories | Imperial Loyalists / Anti-Federationists | Oppose Federation, fear taxation/change, favour local colony autonomy. |
| Declaration of Independence | Draft Constitution / Federation Referendum | Not a military declaration. |
| War of Independence | Referendum and Commonwealth Proclamation sequence | No mandatory war endpoint. |
| Native settlements | First Nations communities on Country | Requires redesign to avoid generic conquest model. |
| Native alarm | Tension / Country Pressure | More nuanced than simple hostility. |
| Native skills | Knowledge Exchange | Unlocked through respectful relations. |
| Missions | Agreement / Mediation / Protectorate-era debates | Avoid direct conversion-as-resource mechanics. |

## Replacement victory loop

### Original FreeCol loop

1. Produce Liberty Bells.
2. Elect Founding Fathers.
3. Reach sufficient Sons of Liberty support.
4. Declare independence.
5. Survive war.

### Australian loop

1. Produce Civic Voice.
2. Recruit Historical Figures.
3. Build colony institutions.
4. Raise Federation Support in each colony region.
5. Draft constitutional clauses.
6. Hold referendums.
7. Negotiate failures or hesitant colonies.
8. Proclaim Commonwealth.

## Why this is not just a reskin

The United States story is about separation through war. Australia's 1901 Federation was a process of joining six colonies into one Commonwealth. The pressure points are therefore different:

- Intercolonial rivalry matters more than anti-monarchy warfare.
- Rail, telegraph, ports, tariffs, and markets matter more than military buildup.
- Civic reform and newspapers matter more than militias.
- First Nations diplomacy and frontier consequences should be persistent, not episodic.

## Father-style perk categories

FreeCol divides Founding Fathers into broad gameplay domains. Australian Mode can keep the structure:

| Category | Role |
|---|---|
| Industry & Commerce | Wool, gold, pastoralism, refrigeration, banking, trade. |
| Exploration & Infrastructure | Coast mapping, inland routes, rivers, telegraph, desert mobility. |
| Settlement, Administration & Defence | Early survival, public works, farming, trade, harbour defence. |
| Democracy & Federation | Federation support, referendums, constitution drafting, conventions. |
| Social Reform & First Nations | Immigration, suffrage, reform, press, diplomacy, frontier relations. |

## Implementation notes

- The existing Founding Father framework can likely be reused structurally.
- Father categories can be renamed internally only at UI layer first.
- Existing point generation could be mapped to five point types.
- Some figures should have hard date gates to avoid anachronism.
- Some figures should have regional requirements so the player cannot unlock everything through one mega-city.
