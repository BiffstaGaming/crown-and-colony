# Historical Figures — Social Reform & First Nations

## Caroline Chisholm

**Category:** Social Reform & First Nations  
**Earliest availability:** 1841+  
**Suggested cost:** 500 Reform points

### Prerequisites

- Immigration system active.
- At least one settlement has labour shortage or gender/family imbalance.
- Port or immigration office exists.
- Housing pressure below critical level.

### Perk — Immigrants’ Friend

- Immigration recruitment pool refreshes 30% faster.
- Assisted Immigrant and Family Settler units are more likely.
- New immigrants cause less unrest if housing and food are available.
- One free Family Settler appears at a port.

### Linked events

- `event.female_immigrants_home`
- `event.assisted_immigration_campaign`

### Story / balance notes

Chisholm should stabilise immigration and town growth. The perk is strongest when the player has prepared housing and food.

## Peter Lalor

**Category:** Social Reform & First Nations  
**Earliest availability:** 1854+  
**Suggested cost:** 600 Reform/Democracy points

### Prerequisites

- Goldfield exists.
- Mining licence unrest, taxation dispute, or policing pressure exists.
- At least one Digger unit or mining town.
- Civic institutions are weak or contested.

### Perk — Eureka Oath

- Unfair licence/tax events generate Federation Support and Reform Pressure instead of only unrest.
- Unlocks `Miner’s Right` reform.
- One goldfield town may raise a limited defensive militia.
- Goldfield unrest recovery improves after reform is enacted.

### Linked events

- `event.eureka_stockade`
- `event.miners_right_reform`

### Story / balance notes

Lalor should convert crisis into democratic reform. Avoid making the Eureka event simply a military combat reward.

## Mary Lee

**Category:** Social Reform & First Nations  
**Earliest availability:** 1888+  
**Suggested cost:** 650 Reform points

### Prerequisites

- South Australia active OR women’s suffrage debate unlocked.
- Newspaper, Town Hall, or Reform Society exists.
- Civic reform score above threshold.
- At least one colony has high Federation Support but low representation legitimacy.

### Perk — Votes for Women

- Town Halls and Newspapers generate +25% Civic Voice.
- South Australia Federation target reduced by 5 points.
- Unlocks `Women’s Suffrage League` reform event.
- Reform Commonwealth victory grade becomes possible.

### Linked events

- `event.womens_suffrage_league`
- `event.south_australian_suffrage`

### Story / balance notes

Lee should make reform a practical path to Federation, not just flavour. Tie her to South Australia and civic institutions.

## Louisa Lawson

**Category:** Social Reform & First Nations  
**Earliest availability:** 1888+  
**Suggested cost:** 600 Reform/Democracy points

### Prerequisites

- Newspaper or Printing Press exists.
- At least one colony has reform movement or low Civic Voice.
- Literacy/schooling or publishing infrastructure active.

### Perk — The Dawn Press

- Newspapers produce +50% Civic Voice.
- Reform events appear 20% more often.
- Female specialist migrants become more likely.
- Anti-Federation apathy decays faster in newspaper towns.

### Linked events

- `event.the_dawn_press`
- `event.women_publishers_and_reform`

### Story / balance notes

Lawson is the press-and-reform figure. She should be especially useful for turning developed towns into Federation Support engines.

## William Barak

**Category:** Social Reform & First Nations  
**Earliest availability:** 1863+ relational unlock; stronger after 1875  
**Suggested cost:** Special: Relationship + Reform points

### Prerequisites

- Kulin/Wurundjeri regional group has been contacted.
- Respect with Kulin/Wurundjeri is at least 70.
- Tension with Kulin/Wurundjeri is below 40.
- No recent attack, forced removal, or broken agreement involving that group.
- Coranderrk or Aboriginal station/protection debate event pool unlocked.

### Perk — Coranderrk Delegation

- Unlocks `Respectful Agreement Council` mechanics.
- First Nations tension decays 25% faster where no new Country pressure is added.
- Country agreements reduce Federation legitimacy penalties.
- Diplomatic missions to First Nations communities produce Reform points instead of conversion-style rewards.

### Linked events

- `event.coranderrk_delegation`
- `event.treaty_council`
- `event.country_agreement_review`

### Story / balance notes

Barak should not feel like a colonial “asset” the player owns. Treat him as a relational diplomacy figure unlocked by respectful play. His perk should reward agreements, voice, and reduced frontier harm.
