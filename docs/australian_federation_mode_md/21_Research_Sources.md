## Design caveats

- The figure perks are game-design interpretations, not claims that the historical people personally caused those effects alone.
- First Nations groups listed are broad design placeholders and should be reviewed by Indigenous sources/consultants before production.
- Federation voting thresholds are game targets inspired by historical referendum outcomes, not exact universal-democratic measures.
- Some events are intentionally marked as optional or sensitive because representing them as normal gameplay events could be inappropriate.
