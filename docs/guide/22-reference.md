# Reference & Colopedia

This closing chapter is your quick-lookup shelf. When you need the exact facts of the world — every terrain, resource, unit, building and Founding Father — the game itself holds the authoritative answer. This chapter tells you where that answer lives, gives you the full keyboard reference, defines the terms used throughout this handbook, and closes with a short note on what Crown & Colony is.

## The in-game Colopedia

Press `C` at any time in the game view to open the **Colopedia** — the game's own encyclopaedia of everything in the world. It is the reference to trust above all others, because it is built directly from the same ruleset data that runs your game. If a value ever changes, the Colopedia changes with it; a hand-written table in a manual can drift out of date, but the Colopedia cannot.

Use it to look up:

- **Terrain** — every land and water type, what it produces, and how it affects movement and defence.
- **Resources** — the special bonuses (like a mineral deposit or a prime fishing ground) that a tile may carry.
- **Goods** — the raw materials and finished products, and which chains turn one into the other.
- **Units** — colonists, soldiers, ships, wagons and artillery, with their roles.
- **Skills and professions** — what each expert specialises in.
- **Buildings** — what each structure does and what it upgrades into.
- **Founding Fathers** — every candidate for your Continental Congress and the lasting benefit each one grants.
- **Nations** — the European powers and the native nations of the New World.

!!! tip
    Get into the habit of opening the Colopedia before a big decision — which good to specialise in, which Father to chase, whether a tile is worth settling. It is faster than guessing and it is never wrong about the current rules.

The chapters in this guide teach you *how the systems work* and *how to play well*, keeping the prose readable rather than crowding it with figures. For the numbers themselves, this handbook collects the most-used reference tables — terrain, goods and prices, buildings, **all 25 Founding Fathers**, unit stats, native nations and difficulty — in **[Tables & Data](23-tables-data.md)**, generated from the same ruleset as the game so they can never drift. For anything not listed there, or the exact figure in the game in front of you, the Colopedia is the final word.

## Keyboard reference

Crown & Colony is played mostly with the **mouse** — click a unit to select it, click a tile to move it or give an order, and click a colony to open its screen. The keyboard adds shortcuts for the common actions. You never have to memorise these: press `F1` in the game to pop up an on-screen legend at any time, and if a key ever shows differently there, it is because you rebound it (see below).

| Key | Action |
|---|---|
| Left-click | Select a unit / move it / open a colony / pick a tile |
| Right-click | Open the tile menu (pick a stacked unit, centre here, go to here) |
| Right/middle drag, mouse wheel, arrow keys | Pan the view / zoom / slide the camera |
| `Enter` | End the turn |
| `Space` | Skip the selected unit for the rest of this turn |
| `W` | Select the next unit that still needs orders this turn |
| `G` | Go to — then click a destination for the unit to travel to over future turns |
| `B` | Build (found) a colony with the selected unit |
| `D` | Disband the selected unit (asks you to confirm first) |
| `E` | Open the Europe screen |
| `L` | Open the *Find settlement* panel |
| `F` | Open the Founding Fathers panel |
| `C` | Open the Colopedia |
| `Ctrl`+`C` | Centre the camera on the selected unit |
| `N` | New game (new map) |
| `Ctrl`+`S` / `Ctrl`+`O` | Save / Load (named save slots) |
| `F5` / `F9` | Quick-save / Quick-load |
| `F1` | Toggle the on-screen key legend |
| `Esc` | Open the pause menu |

!!! tip
    Don't like a key? Open **Settings → Key Bindings…**, click **Rebind** next to a shortcut and press the key you'd rather use — it takes effect straight away and is remembered next time you play. **Reset** restores one shortcut; **Reset all to defaults** restores the whole list.

For the fuller explanation of panning, zooming and the on-screen map controls, see [The Screen & Interface](03-interface.md).

## Glossary

- **High seas** — the deep water at the edges of the map. A ship sailing there leaves for Europe (or returns from it); the voyage takes a few turns. See [Exploration & Discovery](06-exploration.md).
- **Liberty bells** — produced by your colonies' town halls. They raise Sons of Liberty membership and accumulate toward recruiting Founding Fathers. See [Founding Fathers & the Continental Congress](14-founding-fathers.md).
- **Sons of Liberty** — the share of a colony (or of your whole nation) that supports the rebellion. High membership gives a production bonus; a national figure of at least 50% is the gate to declaring independence. See [The Road to Independence](18-road-to-independence.md).
- **Crosses** — produced by churches and chapels; more crosses bring immigrants to your Europe dock faster. See [Trade & the Europe Screen](12-trade-europe.md).
- **Hammers** — the "building material" your colonies generate to complete construction; some builds also need tools. See [Managing a Colony](08-managing-colony.md).
- **REF (Royal Expeditionary Force)** — the King's army, quietly growing back home, that sails in to crush you when you declare independence. See [The King, Taxes & Your Monarch](13-king-taxes.md).
- **Boycott** — a good you may no longer sell in Europe after refusing a tax rise in a "tea party", until you pay the back taxes. See [The King, Taxes & Your Monarch](13-king-taxes.md).
- **Treasure train** — a slow, defenceless unit carrying a fortune in gold, won from a destroyed settlement or a lucky rumour. See [Natives & Diplomacy](15-natives-diplomacy.md).
- **Fog of war** — explored tiles you cannot currently see are shown dimmed and "remembered", hiding enemy movement until you look again. See [The Map & Terrain](04-map-terrain.md).
- **Expert** — a colonist who has mastered a job and does it far better than a plain free colonist. See [Colonists, Professions & Education](09-colonists-education.md).

## A note on faithfulness

Crown & Colony is a fan-made remake that follows the classic *Colonization* ruleset as its primary reference. It is built on the **FreeCol** specification and is released under the **GPL v2** licence. It contains no code, artwork, or data from the original commercial titles — everything here is an independent reimplementation.

Because the game is built from that shared ruleset data, the surest source of truth for any exact figure is always the in-game Colopedia in front of you — and the **[Tables & Data](23-tables-data.md)** appendix is generated from that very same data, so it mirrors the game rather than a hand-copied snapshot.

If you have read this far, you have the whole picture: from your first ship on the high seas to the day the King's fleet appears on the horizon. Go and build a nation.
