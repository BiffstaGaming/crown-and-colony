## Founding Fathers

All 25 fathers and exactly what each one does, grouped by field. You earn them with liberty bells and choose one candidate per field — see [Founding Fathers & the Continental Congress](14-founding-fathers.md).

### Exploration

| Father | Effect |
|---|---|
| **Ferdinand Magellan** | Naval vessels movement is increased by 1 and the time to sail to/from Europe/America is shortened. |
| **Francisco de Coronado** | All existing colonies become visible on the map. |
| **Hernando de Soto** | Exploration of Lost City Rumors never yields a negative result and all land units have an extended sight radius. |
| **Henry Hudson** | Increases output of all Fur trappers by 100%. |
| **Robert de La Salle** | Gives all existing and future colonies a stockade when their population reaches 3. |

### Military

| Father | Effect |
|---|---|
| **Hernan Cortes** | Conquered native settlements always yield treasure (and in greater abundance) and the monarch's galleons transport it free of charge. |
| **George Washington** | Any soldier or dragoon who wins a combat is automatically upgraded to the next possible level. |
| **Paul Revere** | When a colony with no standing soldiers is attacked, a colonist automatically takes up any stockpiled muskets and defends. |
| **Francis Drake** | Increases the combat strength of all Privateers by 50%. |
| **John Paul Jones** | A Frigate is added to your colonial navy (free). |

### Political

| Father | Effect |
|---|---|
| **Thomas Jefferson** | Increases Liberty Bell production in colonies by 50%. |
| **Pocahontas** | All tension levels between you and natives are removed and Indian alarm is generated half as fast. |
| **Thomas Paine** | Increases Liberty Bell production in colonies by the value of the current tax rate. |
| **Simon Bolivar** | Sons of Liberty membership in all existing colonies is increased by 20%. |
| **Benjamin Franklin** | The Crown's foreign wars no longer have effect on relationships in the New World and Europeans in the New World always offer peace in negotiations. |

### Religious

| Father | Effect |
|---|---|
| **William Brewster** | No more criminals or servants appear on the docks, and you can select which immigrant in the recruitment pool to move to the docks. |
| **William Penn** | Cross production in all colonies is increased by 50%. |
| **Father Jean de Brebeuf** | All missionaries function as experts. |
| **Juan de Sepulveda** | Increases the chance that the population of a subjugated Indian settlement will "convert" and join your colonies by 20%. |
| **Bartolome de las Casas** | All existing Indian converts are converted to free colonists. |

### Trade

| Father | Effect |
|---|---|
| **Adam Smith** | Enables building of factories. |
| **Jacob Fugger II** | All boycotts currently in effect are dropped. |
| **Peter Minuit** | Indians no longer demand payment for their land. |
| **Peter Stuyvesant** | The construction of custom houses becomes possible. |
| **Jan de Witt** | Trade with foreign colonies becomes possible and more information about colonial rivals is displayed. |
